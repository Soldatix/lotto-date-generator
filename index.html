About the app

Date Lotto Generator

Generate repeatable lotto combinations from a date, settings and an optional Personal Key.

Version: · Free application

Features

Lotto presets and custom number ranges; copy combinations; save and delete History; light, dark and system themes; five languages.

Privacy & local storage

Your app data stays locally in your browser. This app uses localStorage to remember:

  • History: combinations you save, including the date, settings, numbers and Personal Key.
  • Theme: your light, dark or system preference.
  • Language: your selected interface language.

When you click Save, the Personal Key used to generate that combination is saved with it in localStorage. The Personal Key is not a password or a security secret.

Data / Backup

Export your saved history, language and theme to JSON, or restore them from a backup. Saved Personal Keys are included.
